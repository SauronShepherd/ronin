# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Ronin welcoming, respectful, and
safe for everyone. Contributors are expected to act professionally, assume
good faith, and focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences;
- accepting constructive criticism gracefully; and
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
threats, doxxing, sexualized language or attention, and publishing private
information without consent.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the Ronin repository maintainers
through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting channel. Do not open a public
issue, pull request, or discussion containing sensitive details. If private
reporting is unavailable, use the private maintainer contact described in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports, protect the privacy of everyone involved, and
take the action they consider appropriate, including warnings, temporary or
permanent removal from project spaces, or other measures necessary to keep the
community safe. Reports will be handled as promptly as practicable.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
